How It Works

  1. Roll — a random JoJo character appears with all their stats
  2. Pick ONE aspect — absorbed into your build, no re-rolls
  3. Next character auto-rolls after you pick — up to 8 characters total
  4. Stand users offer their stand stats, ability, and battle item
  5. Non-Stand users offer physical stats and themselves as a companion
  6. Save your build and compare multiple builds by score + synergy
