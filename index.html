Bizarre Adventure · Build Creator

Roll random characters from Parts 1–8. Each roll shows a full character — pick one aspect to absorb. The next character then auto-rolls. Fill up to 12 slots, then save and compare builds.

How It Works

  1. Roll — a random JoJo character appears, all stats hidden
  2. You have 2 Reveals per character — tap any row to uncover its grade
  3. Pick ONE aspect — pick blind or spend reveals first, no re-rolls
  4. Next character auto-rolls after you pick — up to 12 characters total
  5. Stand users offer stand stats, ability, and battle item
  6. Non-Stand users offer physical stats and themselves as a companion (using their companion grade)
  7. Save your build and compare multiple builds by score + synergy
